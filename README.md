# Dynamic map of California's trade in energy sources and uses from the Spanish missions to the present
A map that utilizes Vue 3, TypeScript, and Rsbuild to visualize California’s historical development of trade partners in energy sources and uses. The tool performs geospatial rendering with Leaflet, dynamically projecting and styling national and subnational boundary polygon layers. Beyond local tabular data parsing, the architecture integrates dynamic, asynchronous fetching of XLSX workbooks directly from Google Sheets endpoints, reactively updating spatial data layers and partner classifications as the spreadsheet is updated. To maintain perceptual legibility in monochromatic and grayscale media, the rendering pipeline generates procedural SVG/Canvas texture shaders: diagonal hatching for import flows, stippling for export flows, and composite checkerboard tessellation for bilateral trade partners.
